# Full Paper Outline — Revised Bayesian Triage Program

## Proposed title
*A Calibration-Gated Bayesian Framework for Triage of Anomalous Narrowband Radio Transients: The Wow! Signal as a Case Study*

## Core claim discipline
This paper does not identify the Wow! Signal's origin. It develops and tests an auditable framework, reports which hypotheses are quantitatively comparable under source-supported models, and reports blocked models/data gaps transparently.

## Sections
1. **Introduction:** anomaly triage rather than attribution; scope and preregistered-style evidence boundaries.
2. **Observational record and provenance:** censored flux/bandwidth, two candidate positions, Big Ear timing, follow-up campaigns.
3. **Model architecture:** five-way posterior over marginal evidences; dependency graph; selection effects; model readiness gates.
4. **Geometry and follow-up methods:** Big Ear calibration status; campaign-specific detection functions; restrictions of threshold constraints.
5. **Hypothesis-specific methods:** H1 RFI mixture, H2 ephemeris/beam intersection, H3 flux/trigger/rate feasibility, H4 transmitter population/alignment, H5 restricted stochastic replication.
6. **Validation:** Kipping--Gray asset audit, convergence/discrepancy criteria, synthetic/posterior-predictive tests, benchmark protocol.
7. **Results:** generated observational table, readiness table, H3 flux feasibility, H5 replication status; a five-way posterior only if all gates pass.
8. **Discussion:** model conditionality, limitations, expected information gain from archives/follow-up, reusable API.
9. **Data/code availability and reproducibility.**
