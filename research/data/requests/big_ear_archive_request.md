# Request: 1977 Big Ear technical and observing documentation

**Subject:** Research request for archived Big Ear instrument information relevant to the 15 August 1977 Wow! observation

We are preparing a reproducible, non-attribution study of how anomalous narrowband radio transients should be statistically triaged. We are not seeking to make a definitive origin claim. To prevent unsupported assumptions, we need primary technical documentation for the 1977 Big Ear configuration.

If available, please identify or provide copies/citations for:

1. 1420 MHz beam maps or measured RA/declination FWHM values, including date/configuration and measurement method;
2. two-horn/feed separation, orientation, and the expected time offset for a meridian-transiting point source;
3. receiver/signal-chain configuration, local oscillators, channelization, calibration, and data-recording path in August 1977;
4. observing logs, pointing/coordinate conventions, and original/transcribed detection records;
5. known instrumental artifacts, maintenance events, or local interference diagnostics relevant to that period.

For each record, a stable archival identifier, document date, and any restriction on public release would be valuable. We will preserve provenance and distinguish supplied primary evidence from our own inference.

Thank you for considering this archival research request.
