**H1 — Instrumental/RFI**

* Ehman, J. R., *"The Big Ear Wow\! Signal (30th Anniversary Report)"* — [http://www.bigear.org/Wow30th/wow30th.htm](http://www.bigear.org/Wow30th/wow30th.htm)  
* Méndez et al. (2025), *"Arecibo Wow\! II"* (contains the instrumental-error assessment) — [https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10657](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10657)

**H2 — Cometary hydrogen emission**

* Paris, A. & Davies, E. (2017), *"Hydrogen Line Observations of Cometary Spectra at 1420 MHz"* — [https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03259](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03259)  
* NAAPO's rebuttal of the comet hypothesis — [http://naapo.org/WOWCometRebuttal.html](http://naapo.org/WOWCometRebuttal.html)

**H3 — Interstellar hydrogen cloud / stimulated maser flare**

* Méndez, Ortiz Ceballos & Zuluaga (2024), *"Arecibo Wow\! I: An Astrophysical Explanation for the Wow\! Signal"* — [https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08513](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08513)  
* Méndez et al. (2025), *"Arecibo Wow\! II: Revised Properties of the Wow\! Signal"* — [https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10657](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10657)  
* Project landing page with figures/data summary — [https://phl.upr.edu/wow](https://phl.upr.edu/wow)

**H4 — Artificial interstellar power beam (leakage)**

* Benford, J. (2021), *"Was the Wow Signal Due to Power Beaming Leakage?"*, JBIS 74:196–200 — the journal version is paywalled with no free preprint I could find; Benford's own public summary of the same argument is here: [https://www.centauri-dreams.org/2021/01/22/was-the-wow-signal-due-to-power-beaming-leakage/](https://www.centauri-dreams.org/2021/01/22/was-the-wow-signal-due-to-power-beaming-leakage/)  
* Benford (2025), *"Beaming and Bandwidth"* — [https://www.centauri-dreams.org/2025/09/22/beaming-and-bandwidth-a-new-note-on-the-wow-signal/](https://www.centauri-dreams.org/2025/09/22/beaming-and-bandwidth-a-new-note-on-the-wow-signal/)

**H5 — Stochastic repeating ETI beacon**

* Kipping, D. & Gray, R. (2022), *"Could the 'Wow' signal have originated from a stochastic repeating beacon?"*, MNRAS — [https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08374](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08374)

One honest flag: the JBIS-published version of Benford (2021) is the version of record you'd cite formally, but I couldn't find it free anywhere — you may need institutional/library access or to email Benford directly for a copy when you get to writing.
